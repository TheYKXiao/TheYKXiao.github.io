## The (Long) Road to Computer Graphics Mastery

For a while, one of my dream career paths was to create CG environments for games or animation. With artistic talent that is, let us say, limited, I decided to turn to the more technical side of graphics.

So, amid the usual chaos of Ph.D. life, I took the first step on the road to mastery by taking CSCI 5607 - Fundamentals of Computer Graphics to build a theoretical foundation! Hopefully, the world will eventually have one more graphics master (possibly in about twenty years).
